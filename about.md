I'm **Awadhesh Kumar**, a Senior Technical Lead based in Bangalore, with around 15
years in **cloud infrastructure** — Ericsson CEE (Cloud Execution Environment),
OpenStack, Kubernetes, and software-defined networking (OVS, VXLAN, EVPN). Much of my
work has been large-scale troubleshooting, system integration, and turning complex
customer and operational problems into scalable tooling.

More recently I've designed and built **multi-agent AI systems** used internally —
"SOLVE" for federated knowledge retrieval across Jira, docs, and knowledge bases, and
"LogIQ" for automated log analysis and root-cause identification — work that
meaningfully cut troubleshooting time.

That led me to an **AI-native learning journey**: figuring out how AI changes the way
we design, build, and maintain software. Along the way I've been practicing
**spec-driven development** — writing the intent down first, keeping it versioned next
to the code, and treating specs and docs as **living artifacts** rather than snapshots
that rot.

I build small open-source tools to learn in the open (see my
[GitHub](https://github.com/awadhesh246)), and I write up what I learn here and on
[my blog](https://awadhesh246.wordpress.com).
